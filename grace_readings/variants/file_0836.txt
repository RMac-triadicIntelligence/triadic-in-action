TASK
Describe grace as the four Gospels show it: Matthew, Mark, Luke and John.
Use ONLY these four books. Do not use Paul's letters, any other New Testament
writing, later theology, commentary or church tradition. If something you would
say comes from outside the four Gospels, leave it out.


Grace appears both where the word itself is used and where the pattern is shown in
parables, healings, meals, calls and acts of forgiveness. Read both.


For each property of grace you find:
1. State it in one plain sentence.
2. Cite chapter and verse, at least two passages where possible.
3. Note any Gospel passage that pulls the other way. Do not smooth over tensions
between passages.
4. Tag the claim TEXT (stated directly in the passage) or READING (your
interpretation).


Cover at least these questions:
- Where does grace come from? Can a person give it to themselves?
- When does it come, relative to asking, confession or repentance?
- Does it go to everyone alike, or does it seek out the one?
- Is it given in the same measure to all, or does it vary?
- Does it carry direction, truth or a call, or only release?
- Can it be refused, withheld, lost or taken back?
- Does it flow from one person who received it to others?
- Why do different people receive it differently? Can a person's capacity to
receive it change, and what changes it?
- Does it restore standing or identity, or only permit something?
- How does it relate to forgiveness, mercy and love?


Then list what grace is NOT in these texts.


Last, and kept separate from the reading: for each property, one line on what it
would require of an artificial developing organism. Label this section
INTERPRETATION.
