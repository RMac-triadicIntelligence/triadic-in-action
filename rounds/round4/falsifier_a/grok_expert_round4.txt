R4-F1 — §1.3, §7.4–§7.7 (two-key gate + barrier-height prediction) Concrete failure: Reference dynamics use gradient of V(x,h)=h(x²−1)² plus bounded input. Larger h increases restoring force, so from any fixed “ready” pre-crossing state the probability (or deterministic success) of basin transition falls with h.
	•	Implementer who respects §1.2 (barrier unchanged) and ordinary U produces h-dependent success → prediction fails, contradicting the “predeclared model property.”
	•	Implementer who forces independence (extra h-proportional kick only when Grace∧Readiness, or gate-driven state jump) either lowers/ignores the barrier or lets the grantor/gate choose direction, violating §1.2 and §1.9. Negative-control model is separate; main arm has no rule that achieves independence without keel breach. Prediction therefore cannot pass or fail for the right reasons. Severity: WOUNDING Minimal fix: Give an explicit update (or extra readiness-owned energy/noise term independent of h) plus a short proof that P(cross | ready, grace) is constant across the frozen h-set without altering V; or retract the independence claim and score only “cross iff both keys” at fixed h. Source: DOC
