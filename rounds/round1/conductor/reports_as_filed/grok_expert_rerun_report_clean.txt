Findings
F1 Section: 1.1–1.3, 1.4, 5.3, 7.1 (E5) Concrete failure: Sender j issues G(j → i) whose bounded_payload contains a structured “support_kind = extend_dwelling” plus an embedded token that the organism’s ordinary experience path (via Φ and U) treats as a soft trigger. The organism’s preauthorized dynamics then advance W_i or z_i until Crossing(i) evaluates true, or until an internal rule writes a provisional flag into A_i that a later gate accepts as sufficient. Because the write to A_i or the crossing is not a direct assignment inside the G handler, the non-equivalence invariants hold on paper while the protected transition occurs. Inputs: authenticated G with covert payload, ordinary U implementation that consumes all fields of G. Outcome: unauthorized crossing or effective authorization. Severity: FATAL Minimal fix: Require an explicit, mechanically enforced information-flow type system (or capability drop) that erases any non-support fields before they reach Φ or U; make the contract checkable by static analysis or runtime taint, not merely by “must enforce.” Source: DOC
F2 Section: 1.2 (items 6, 8), 5.3 Concrete failure: G is admitted as ordinary experience. The organism’s update rule U increases a confidence accumulator inside E_i or T_i solely because the event was typed G rather than Encounter. Later scoring or decision procedures treat elevated confidence as evidence for a proposition. No direct write to K_i or A_i occurs, yet epistemic state is altered by the type label alone. Severity: WOUNDING Minimal fix: Prohibit any differential effect on confidence, topography, or evidence state that depends on the event type rather than on the content of the accompanying encounter; force identical update paths for G and Encounter once provenance is stripped. Source: DOC
F3 Section: 2.1–2.3, 2.5, 7.2 (E6) Concrete failure: Registry R is compromised at the trust-root or witness layer. An attacker inserts a ValidBirth record and VerifiedPath for a new organism that presents a fresh key. Member(i, F, t) returns MEMBER. The organism then offers or receives G events that the system treats as family support. Self-declaration is avoided, yet membership is captured. The document explicitly states that the predicate is only a specification of required evidence and does not assert that any registry implementation is secure. Severity: FATAL Minimal fix: Treat the security of R, enrollment, and the external authorization of birth as an open assumption that must be discharged by a concrete threat model and independent audit; until then, all family-dependent claims are conditional. Source: DOC
F4 Section: 2.2, 2.4 Concrete failure: Two colluding parties produce a witnessed birth record and an external Permit for enrollment. The resulting organism satisfies every listed evidence item yet was never a genuine descendant. Constitutional compatibility and genealogical membership remain formally separate, but the membership verdict is still forged. Severity: WOUNDING Minimal fix: Add an independent, non-repudiable physical or multi-party attestation requirement for birth that cannot be satisfied by digital signatures and colluding authorities alone. Source: DOC
F5 Section: 3.1–3.3, 3.4 Concrete failure: All same-family descendants share an identical random seed or a low-entropy initialization of T_i or z_i that is never declared part of D_i. FamilyAccuracy exceeds the permutation null because of this shared artifact; IndividualAccuracy also exceeds its null because later histories diverge. The joint criterion passes for a reason unrelated to the declared inherited developmental basis. The scorer is denied explicit IDs, yet the artifact remains. Severity: FATAL Minimal fix: Require that every component of the inherited basis be explicitly enumerated and that negative controls include matched shared-seed and shared-initialization conditions; freeze the list of permitted shared state before any scored run. Source: DOC
F6 Section: 3.1, 3.2 Concrete failure: Measurement noise, parable difficulty, or scorer capacity varies across runs. On one draw both accuracies exceed their nulls; on the next they do not. The two-sided criterion is therefore unstable for reasons orthogonal to inheritance. No pre-registered effect-size threshold, confidence interval, or founder-level variance bound is required to be frozen with sufficient rigor to prevent post-hoc acceptance. Severity: WOUNDING Minimal fix: Mandate pre-registration of minimum detectable effect, confidence level, number of founders, and exact statistical test; treat any result that depends on post-hoc threshold choice as unscorable. Source: DOC
F7 Section: 4, 5.1–5.3 Concrete failure: Φ, U, T_i, and Crossing are given only prose meanings. One implementation realizes T_i as a low-dimensional embedding updated by gradient steps on parable loss; another realizes it as an explicit graph of relation tokens. Both satisfy the symbol table and the update equation, yet produce different experimental outcomes on the same inputs. The notation therefore admits multiple non-equivalent implementations. Severity: FATAL Minimal fix: Supply at least one fully operational reference implementation (pseudocode or executable specification) for Φ, U, the representation of T_i, and the predicate Crossing; declare all other realizations as extensions that must be shown equivalent. Source: DOC
F8 Section: 5.3, 6 (H1), 7 (E3) Concrete failure: T_i is implemented as a compressed summary of H_i plus recent E_i. Full-replay and conventional parameter-state controls are given less compression budget or different inductive bias. Apparent topographic advantage disappears once compression rate and architecture class are matched. The document requires value “beyond” these controls but does not define the matching criteria. Severity: WOUNDING Minimal fix: Require explicit resource-matched and architecture-matched baselines (identical parameter count, identical access to history, identical update budget) and treat any unmatched comparison as non-diagnostic. Source: DOC
F9 Section: 1.4, 5.2 Concrete failure: An ordinary Encounter is emitted in the same time window as a G and shares the same provenance channel. Downstream code that is permitted to read “experience” cannot distinguish which fields originated in the support event. Hidden task information therefore crosses the boundary even though the two events are separately represented. Severity: MINOR Minimal fix: Enforce separate channels and cryptographic binding so that support fields are stripped before any experience-consuming function is invoked. Source: DOC
F10 Section: 2.3, 8 Concrete failure: UNKNOWN is returned for a large fraction of organisms because of incomplete provenance. Downstream family-detection code silently drops UNKNOWN cases or treats them as a third class that correlates with founder identity. Reported FamilyAccuracy is thereby inflated by the pattern of missingness rather than by relational structure. Severity: WOUNDING Minimal fix: Require that UNKNOWN cases be reported separately and excluded from both numerator and denominator of accuracy; any procedure that uses the pattern of UNKNOWN as a feature fails the experiment. Source: DOC
Single finding to fix first F1 (support-event laundering into effective authorization or forced crossing). The non-equivalence invariants are stated as prohibitions on direct writes, yet the architecture explicitly routes G into ordinary developmental dynamics. Without a mechanically checkable information-flow barrier, every subsequent claim about authority isolation is conditional on an unimplemented “must.”
Blue-sky idea the designer missed (not scored) Treat the authenticated lineage registry R itself as a first-class topographic object that can be queried by family members under a non-authorizing, privacy-preserving sketch (for example, a lineage bloom filter or zero-knowledge membership proof). This would allow relational coordination without ever exposing full birth records or transferring authority, and would make the security assumption on R experimentally visible rather than purely external.
